TailScreen

Your Mac on your iPad — fast, sharp and direct over Tailscale.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPad and Mac.

⚡

Hardware video

HEVC or H.264 encoded on your Mac’s media engine, up to 120 fps, with adaptive quality.

🖐️

Made for touch

Scroll by dragging, pinch to zoom, long-press to select. Or switch to trackpad mode.

⌨️

Keyboard and trackpad

Magic Keyboard, mouse and trackpad work as on a Mac, plus a key bar with ⌘ ⌥ ⌃ and arrows.

📋

Shared clipboard

Copy on the Mac, paste on the iPad – and send iPad text to the Mac with one tap.

🔒

Private by design

Your screen goes straight from your Mac to your iPad over your own Tailscale network. No accounts, no servers.

🌍

English and Bulgarian

The whole app is available in both languages.

How it works

  1. Install Tailscale on your iPad and your Mac and sign in to the same tailnet.
  2. Install TailScreen on both – one purchase covers iPad and Mac.
  3. On the Mac, allow Screen Recording and Accessibility when TailScreen asks.
  4. Scan the QR code on the Mac with the iPad’s Camera. Later connections need no PIN.
